THE BHADRACHALAM EXPERIENCE

More Than a Temple Town

Bhadrachalam brings together spirituality, heritage, traditional stories, the Godavari River and natural landscapes. Explore some of the places that form part of the region's tourism and cultural experience.

01 • SACRED LANDMARK

Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy Temple

The temple is the spiritual centerpiece of Bhadrachalam and is dedicated to Lord Sri Rama, Goddess Sita and Lakshmana.

Its devotional traditions, architecture and association with Bhakta Ramadasu make it an important part of the cultural identity of Bhadrachalam.
