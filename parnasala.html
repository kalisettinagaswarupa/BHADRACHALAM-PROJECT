A Place of Stories, Faith and Natural Beauty

Local religious traditions connect Parnasala with episodes associated with the forest journey of Lord Rama, Sita and Lakshmana.

These associations form part of the devotional storytelling and cultural heritage of the region. They should be understood as traditional beliefs rather than independently verified historical events.

TRADITIONAL NARRATIVE

Parnasala in the Ramayana Tradition

According to local religious tradition, Parnasala is associated with the period when Rama, Sita and Lakshmana lived in the forest during their exile.

Stories connected with the region are preserved through devotional traditions, local landmarks, sculptures and visitor narratives.

Tradition Note

The Ramayana associations described here are traditional religious beliefs and cultural narratives, not claims of archaeologically established historical events.
