Welcome to Bhadrachalam

A Sacred Town by the Godavari

Bhadrachalam is a pilgrimage town in Telangana known for the Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy Temple and its setting along the Godavari River.

Where Faith Meets Heritage

Bhadrachalam is closely associated with the worship of Sri Rama and is home to the Sri Sita Ramachandra Swamy Temple.

The Godavari River, temple architecture, devotional traditions and surrounding landscapes create a distinctive cultural setting.

The Bhadrachalam region is also associated with Ramayana traditions, particularly around Parnasala.
